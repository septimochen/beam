## A · Prism Beam

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: an original app-icon concept presentation for Beam, a private peer-to-peer file, text and URL transfer app on macOS and Android. Create ONE polished landscape concept board, 1536x1024 composition, exceptionally clean contemporary product design. Show TWO large identical copies of the proposed macOS app icon, one centered in the left half on a warm light-gray wallpaper, the other centered in the right half on a deep charcoal wallpaper. Each icon must be the SAME artwork and colorway across backgrounds. Rounded-square macOS app tile, strong clear silhouette, careful optical centering, substantial negative space, refined soft highlights, subtle physical depth, restrained professional finish. No enclosing device mockups, no extra application chrome, no decorative scenery, no wordmarks or text inside the icon, no arrows from a stock share icon, no padlock or folder. The symbol must remain recognizable when reduced to Dock size. The symbol must be separable into foreground/background and also have a viable solid monochrome silhouette for an Android adaptive/themed icon and a macOS menu-bar template. At the top left use only the exact concept title provided. Beneath each icon, small neutral captions exactly 'Light' and 'Dark'. Design directions are exploratory, not a final production export.
Exact concept title: "A · Prism Beam". Subject: a broad luminous diagonal beam of cyan-white light travels from lower-left to upper-right THROUGH two simple softly beveled upright endpoints, symbolizing a direct fast transfer between two devices. Design a distinctive balanced geometric mark, not a lightning bolt, not a letter Z, not chevrons. Make the light beam a solid readable piece of geometry with a bright face and a restrained cyan edge; endpoints are translucent azure solid forms. Deep midnight-blue ceramic rounded-square tile with a subtle blue lift at its center. Cyan and icy blue palette, very subtle glow only as a secondary accent, premium almost tactile macOS appearance. Favor a strong dynamic beam silhouette with few large shapes and no visual noise.
```
